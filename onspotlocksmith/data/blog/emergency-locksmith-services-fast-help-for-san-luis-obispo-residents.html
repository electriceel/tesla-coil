Preparing for a Locksmith Emergency

While unexpected, there are steps you can take to mitigate the impact of a locksmith emergency:
Save Our Number: Store OnSpot Locksmith’s contact information in your phone for immediate access during emergencies.
Secure a Spare Key: Arrange for a spare key to be accessible through a trusted neighbor or hidden securely on your property.
Know Your Locks: Familiarize yourself with the types and brands of locks installed on your property to provide accurate information to the locksmith.
Prioritize Safety: If you’re locked out, especially at night, find a safe, well-lit area to wait for our arrival.
